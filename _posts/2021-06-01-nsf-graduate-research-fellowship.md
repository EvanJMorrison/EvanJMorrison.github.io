My National Science Foundation Graduate Research Fellowship supported my doctoral research at the University of Colorado Anschutz Medical Campus from June 2021 through August 2022.

The project, **Primary protein sequence regulates gene expression**, was part of my PhD work in Olivia S. Rissland's lab. It sits within the question that shaped my doctoral research: how does the sequence at the beginning of a protein influence its fate?

**Fellowship:** NSF GRFP-2020300571.
