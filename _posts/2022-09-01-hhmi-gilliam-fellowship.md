My Howard Hughes Medical Institute Gilliam Fellowship began in September 2022, during my PhD in Molecular Biology & Biochemistry at the University of Colorado Anschutz Medical Campus.

The supported project, **A tripeptide motif induces rapid protein decay in human cells**, investigated how short sequence features influence protein stability. I carried out this work in Olivia S. Rissland's lab as part of my research on N-degron pathways.

**Fellowship:** HHMI Gilliam Fellowship, GT14919.
