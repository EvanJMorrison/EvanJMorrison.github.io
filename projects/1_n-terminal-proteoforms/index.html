N-terminal proteoform diversity

How alternative N-termini arise and what they do to protein fate.

A single gene can give rise to proteins with different N-termini — through alternative translation initiation, co-translational processing by methionine aminopeptidases, and downstream modification. These N-terminal differences are not cosmetic: they help determine whether a protein is stabilized or targeted for degradation.

This thread reviews where that diversity comes from and what it costs the cell (Morrison & Rissland, 2025).
