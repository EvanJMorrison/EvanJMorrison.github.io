I have been awarded a **National Science Foundation Graduate Research Fellowship** to support my doctoral research at the University of Colorado Anschutz Medical Campus.

The project, **Primary protein sequence regulates gene expression**, is part of my PhD work in Olivia S. Rissland's lab. It sits within the question driving my doctoral research: how does the sequence at the beginning of a protein influence its fate?

**Fellowship:** NSF GRFP-2020300571.
