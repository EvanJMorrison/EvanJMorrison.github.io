I have officially earned my **PhD in Molecular Biology & Biochemistry** from the University of Colorado Anschutz Medical Campus.

I began the program in 2018 and carried out my doctoral research with **Olivia S. Rissland**. My thesis project was **Discovery and characterization of a MetAP-dependent N-degron pathway**.

My PhD work examined how N-terminal sequence features and processing influence protein degradation. It also included work on N-terminal proteoform diversity and the characterization of 2A peptide-bond-skipping sequences.

Along the way, my training was supported by an NIH T32 fellowship, the [NSF Graduate Research Fellowship]({{ '/news/2021/nsf-graduate-research-fellowship/' | relative_url }}), and the [HHMI Gilliam Fellowship]({{ '/news/2022/hhmi-gilliam-fellowship/' | relative_url }}).
