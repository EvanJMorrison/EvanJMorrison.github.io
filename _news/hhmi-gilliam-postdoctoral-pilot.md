HHMI has announced a **postdoctoral pilot phase of the Gilliam Fellowship**, extending its support beyond the PhD. Through this pilot, my fellowship will fund **four years of my postdoctoral research**.

This support will help me build on my doctoral training and pursue new questions about ribosome and protein quality control as I take the next step in my scientific career.

[About the HHMI Gilliam Fellows Program](https://www.hhmi.org/programs/gilliam-fellows).
