I was officially awarded an **HHMI Gilliam Fellowship**, which supports PhD students in the life sciences through research funding, professional development, and a scientific community committed to strong mentorship and inclusive training environments. The program also provides mentorship training for fellows' PhD advisors.

This fellowship will support my PhD in Molecular Biology & Biochemistry at the University of Colorado Anschutz Medical Campus, where I work with **Olivia S. Rissland**.

The supported project, **A tripeptide motif induces rapid protein decay in human cells**, investigates how short sequence features influence protein stability as part of my research on N-degron pathways.

**Fellowship:** HHMI Gilliam Fellowship, GT14919.
