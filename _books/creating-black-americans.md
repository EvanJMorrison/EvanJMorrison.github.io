If European Americans genuinely want to contend with the America they’ve created, then this book is a must-read. It provides a cursory glance of black life since it was created on the slave ships and ends with contemporary black politics.

The book is good at dispelling common myths surrounding slavery and the Jim Crow Era, and to the critical reader offers insights into contemporary black plight. While this is a great book for beginners, it is only that: an introduction to the history of black life in the USA.
